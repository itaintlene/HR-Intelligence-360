# Code of Conduct

## 1. Professional Behaviour
Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying of any kind are prohibited.

## 2. Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial or family interest that may conflict with the company's interests to their manager and HR within 5 working days of becoming aware of it.

## 3. Confidential Information
Confidential company and employee information must not be shared outside the company without written authorisation. This obligation continues after employment ends.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality
Employees may not accept gifts worth more than 50 USD from vendors or customers. Gifts above this limit must be declined or reported to HR.

## 5. Reporting Concerns
Concerns about misconduct can be raised with HR or through the confidential ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 6. Disciplinary Process
Violations are investigated by HR. Disciplinary outcomes are decided by HR leadership following a documented process; no automated system makes disciplinary decisions.
