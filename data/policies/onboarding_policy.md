# Onboarding and Documentation Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines the documents and tasks required to onboard a new employee so that HR records are complete and compliant.

## 2. Mandatory Onboarding Documents
Every new employee must submit the following seven documents: signed offer letter, government ID proof, address proof, tax declaration form, signed non-disclosure agreement (NDA), bank details, and education certificates.

## 3. Submission Deadline
All mandatory documents must be submitted within 7 calendar days of the joining date. Documents not received after 7 days are escalated by HR to the employee and the reporting manager.

## 4. Identity and Expiry Rules
Government ID proof must be valid on the date of submission. HR must request a renewed ID proof from employees whose ID expires within 30 days or has already expired.

## 5. Probation Period
The standard probation period is 90 days from the joining date. Probation confirmation is a decision made by the reporting manager and HR leadership; it is never automated.

## 6. First-Week Tasks
Within the first week HR ensures that IT equipment and accounts are provisioned, a manager introduction is held, and a 30-60-90 day plan is shared. New employees complete the policy orientation within 14 days of joining.

## 7. Record Accuracy
HR must keep the employee master record accurate. Mandatory master data fields are employee ID, name, email, department, designation, joining date and employment status. Each employee email address must be unique.
