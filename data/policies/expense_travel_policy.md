# Expense and Travel Policy

## 1. Scope
This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses incurred while performing company work.

## 2. Pre-Approval of Travel
All business travel must be approved by the reporting manager before booking. Air travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible.

## 3. Daily Allowance
Domestic travel attracts a daily meal allowance of 30 USD. International travel attracts a daily meal allowance of 60 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 4. Submitting Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date together with original receipts. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected.

## 5. Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are reimbursed within 15 working days. Claims above 500 USD require additional approval from the department head.
