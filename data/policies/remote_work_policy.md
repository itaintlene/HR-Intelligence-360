# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period and whose role allows remote work may apply for a hybrid or remote arrangement.

## 2. Hybrid Schedule
The standard hybrid arrangement requires employees to work from the office at least 3 days per week. Teams may agree on specific anchor days with their manager.

## 3. Approval
Remote work arrangements must be approved by the reporting manager and recorded by HR. Fully remote arrangements additionally require approval from the department head.

## 4. Working Hours and Availability
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 11:00 to 16:00 local time and respond to messages within a reasonable time.

## 5. Equipment and Allowance
The company provides a laptop. A monthly internet allowance of 40 USD is paid to employees on an approved fully remote arrangement. Home-office furniture is not reimbursed.

## 6. Data Security
Remote employees must use the company VPN, keep devices encrypted and must not store company or employee data on personal devices.
