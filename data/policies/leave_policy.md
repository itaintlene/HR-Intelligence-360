# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose and Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees of Example Corp (a fictional company). It defines leave entitlements, how to apply, and how leave is approved.

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlement
Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 days of paid leave per calendar year, made up of 12 casual leave days, 8 earned leave days and 4 floating holidays. Entitlements are credited on 1 January. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rata entitlement.

## 3. Casual Leave
Casual leave is meant for short, unplanned absences. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days may be taken at a time. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

## 4. Earned Leave
Earned leave is meant for planned time off. Employees must apply at least 7 calendar days in advance for earned leave of more than 2 days. Up to 10 unused earned leave days may be carried forward to the next calendar year. Earned leave can be encashed only at separation.

## 5. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be encashed or carried forward.

## 6. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth of the child.

## 7. Leave Application and Approval
Leave must be requested through the HR portal. The reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days. HR does not approve leave on behalf of managers. Leave taken without approval is treated as unpaid leave.

## 8. Public Holidays
The company publishes a list of 12 public holidays at the start of every year. Public holidays falling within a leave period are not counted as leave days.
